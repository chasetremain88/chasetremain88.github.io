01

PROJECT OVERVIEW

The objective of this academic project was to design and build a mechanical linkage capable of controlling the position and motion of a laser.

The project involved translating the desired laser motion into a mechanical system that could be modeled, manufactured, assembled, and experimentally tested.

02

LINKAGE DESIGN

Link geometry and component dimensions were developed around the required motion of the laser. The relationship between linkage input and laser position was considered throughout the mechanical design process.

03

PROTOTYPE & TESTING

The final mechanism was assembled and tested to evaluate its physical motion and compare the completed system with the intended linkage behavior.
