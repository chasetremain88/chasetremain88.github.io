PROJECT OVERVIEW

During my internship at Cincinnati Fan, I worked on the development and optimization of robotic welding processes using a Yaskawa ArcWorld 2000 robotic welding system.

My work included robot programming, process optimization, fixture design, and analysis of welding parameters to improve manufacturing efficiency and expand robotic welding capabilities.

02

ROBOTIC WELDING

I programmed and optimized the robotic welding system to improve existing operations and develop new automated welding processes. Process improvements reduced robotic welding cycle time by 8%.

03

PROCESS DEVELOPMENT

I programmed the first robotically welded wheel at Cincinnati Fan, developing an automated process that reduced direct labor requirements by 80%.

Wire feed speed, travel speed, heat input, and part geometry were evaluated to establish appropriate welding parameters for the automated process.

04

FIXTURE DESIGN

Custom welding and testing fixtures were designed using CAD and mechanical design principles to improve positioning, repeatability, and manufacturing process development.
